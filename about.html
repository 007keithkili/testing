About RemoteTravels in Narok

RemoteTravels, your gateway to authentic Maasai Mara adventures and the heart of Narok Town. We've been serving intrepid explorers, wildlife enthusiasts, and culture seekers since 2022, crafting seamless safari experiences that begin the moment you touch down in Narok.

Why Narok?

Situated just 80 km from the iconic Maasai Mara, Narok Town is the perfect launchpad for game drives, hot-air balloon rides, and cultural immersions. From the lively Market Roundabout to the serene lodge ecosystems, we ensure you feel right at home—no matter where your journey takes you.

Our Narok office is staffed by local experts who know the Mara's wildlife corridors, cultural nuances, and hidden gems like the back of their hand. We're here to make your safari dreams a reality, whether you're a first-time visitor or a seasoned traveler.

Ensuring Your Safety & Peace of Mind

  • Verified Vehicles & Drivers: All our 4×4s undergo daily inspections; drivers are licensed, Maasai-trained professionals with in-depth knowledge of the Mara's wildlife corridors.
  • Real-Time Tracking: Each vehicle is GPS-monitored and linked to our 24/7 dispatch center in Narok, so we always know where you are—even off the beaten path.
  • Emergency Response Protocols: Whether it's a flat tire or a sudden storm, our rapid-deployment support teams are on standby at both Narok town and lodge locations.
  • Health & Hygiene Standards: From contactless check-ins to sanitized cabins and hand-washing stations at every touchpoint, your well-being is our top priority.
